Nibble Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Your logs stay on your device

Nibble records the calories, optional macros, notes and water amounts you enter. They are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We do not run servers, and we never receive, read or sell your food or water logs.

Camera and food scanning

If you use Scan food, Nibble asks for camera access. The photo and LiDAR depth data are analysed on your device with Apple's Vision and ARKit frameworks to recognise the food and estimate the portion. They are not saved, uploaded or shared. Only the calories and nutrition you confirm are stored in your log.

Notifications

If you turn on hydration reminders, Nibble asks for notification permission and schedules local reminders on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Advertising

Nibble is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Where required, you're asked for consent first, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you decline tracking. Your logs are never shared with advertisers.

Backups and deletion

Your entries are included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on. You can delete individual entries, or everything at once from Settings. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device.

Not medical advice

Nibble is a simple logging tool and does not provide medical or nutritional advice.

Children

Nibble is not directed at children under 13.
