Frequently asked questions

How does food scanning work?

Tap Scan, point the camera at your meal and tap the shutter. Nibble recognises the food on your device and, on iPhone Pro models with LiDAR, measures the portion. You can change the food, portion and calories before anything is added. Without LiDAR, choose a photo and adjust the portion by hand.

How accurate are scanned calories?

They are estimates, usually within about 20–30%. Always review the result and adjust it. Nibble is not medical or nutritional advice.

What does the ad unlock?

Watching a short ad unlocks weekly calorie, macro and water graphs plus hydration reminders for 24 hours, or adds 3 more scans for the day. Everyday logging is always free and never needs an ad.

I'm not getting hydration reminders.

Turn them on in Settings, and check iOS Settings → Notifications → Nibble. Reminders are part of the unlock and pause when it ends.

Can I change my goals or use ounces?

Yes. Open Settings to change the calorie goal, water goal, glass size and units.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. See the Privacy Policy.
